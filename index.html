House rules
  • Courts fill in arrival order: first four on Tier 1, last four in the gulag. Up to 4 subs wait on the gulag.
  • Starting matchups are random.
  • Win and you level up a tier. Lose and you drop a tier.
  • Win on Tier 1 and you stay on the court.
  • Lose in the gulag and you’re subbed off with your teammate.
  • King of the court (trial): from round 3 the pair that wins Tier 1 stays together as the kings until they lose. The Tier 2 winners come up as a pair to challenge them. A ref can switch it on or off any round.
  • Losing in the gulag costs points in the seeding (from round 3 on).
  • With 3 subs, the pair who just lost in the gulag play a best-of-3 point slam while they sit out. The winner takes the second sub spot next round.
  • After every game you face your old teammate: whether you went up, came down, stayed on Tier 1 or subbed back on. Flip the racket or pick the teams yourself; either way you partner someone from the other pair.
  • Games are first to 3. Scores feed tonight’s ranking and the overall seedings.
